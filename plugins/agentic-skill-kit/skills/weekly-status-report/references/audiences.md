# Audience Profiles

| Audience | Wants | Include | Leave out | Max length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Manager** | What moved, what's stuck, what needs me | All sections, owners, ETAs, blockers, decisions needed | Low-level task detail | ~350 words |
| **Leadership** | Status at a glance, risks, asks | TL;DR, overall RAG, top 3 metrics, top risks, decisions needed | Task lists, individual names except owners of Red items | ~150 words |
| **Team** | Who does what next | Done, In progress with owners, Next week, blockers | Commercial/sensitive info | ~350 words |
| **Client** | Progress on *their* deliverables, next steps | Milestones, dates, what we need from them | Internal blockers, internal names/tools, blame, internal RAG detail | ~250 words |

## Tone
- Manager/Team: direct, specific, no fluff.
- Leadership: outcome-first, numbers, one clear ask.
- Client: professional, positive but honest. Frame issues as "what we're doing about it".
